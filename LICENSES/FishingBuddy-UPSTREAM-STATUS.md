## Scope

This record is intentionally conservative.

It does not attempt to determine whether a particular historical release had
additional permissions that are no longer shown on current hosting pages.

If reliable historical permission from Sutorix or another applicable rights
holder is later located, preserve that evidence here and update
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Do not use a third-party mirror repository's own software license as proof that
the mirrored Fishing Buddy addon itself was released under that license.
